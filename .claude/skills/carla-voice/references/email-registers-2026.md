# Email registers, July–August 2026

Verbatim extracts from her sent mail. **Coachee names, their circumstances and
the substance of their coaching are removed** – this repository is public and
none of that belongs in it. What is kept is the shape of her sentences.

---

## Coaching follow-up, after a session

> Dear [coachee]
>
> Thank you again for showing up so fully today.
>
> I have updated the *Activities Sheet:* […]
>
> You will find the [practice] there (and pasted below), and really looking
> forward to hearing what that unlocks in your life over the next week. Do also
> continue with the self-observation.
>
> Finally, you will notice I have added two lines in blue when it comes to the
> outcomes of what we're working towards together. Very curious to explore how
> these land and to refine further next time.
>
> Go well and take care,
>
> Carla xxx

Same register, other sessions: *"Gently checking in after the weekend […] As
promised, I've written up the activities we discussed, with a few refinements."*
And: *"Thank you for the lovely session. Herewith the notes on the activities.
Looking forward to what happens for you, Carla"*

"Gently checking in" recurs across several of these. "Herewith" is hers. So is a
subject line ending in ":)".

## Cold introduction to someone senior she wants to learn from

> Good afternoon [name]
>
> It is a real pleasure to connect with you, [referrer] is my coaching pod
> mentor, has shared your details, and I hope you don't mind me reaching out.
>
> Over the past two years, I have found myself increasingly drawn to the work of
> death doulas and the broader field of end-of-life care. It is something I
> would like to explore more deeply and thoughtfully.
>
> I am currently completing the Professional Coaching Course at the UCT Graduate
> School of Business, and I have been exploring the intersection between
> coaching, presence, and end-of-life support. I would truly value the
> opportunity to meet with you, if you would be open to it; simply to hear more
> about your journey, your work, and any insights you might be willing to share.
>
> Thank you for considering it, and I hope we have the opportunity to connect.
>
> Warm regards,
> Carla

No pitch and no ask beyond a conversation. States the interest plainly, gives
the credential once, asks for nothing but time.

## Saying no to a paid offer

> Hi [name],
>
> Thank you so much, we really appreciate your enthusiasm and the offer.
>
> As this is still a very small event, we're unfortunately not in a position to
> invest in paid promotion at this stage. A basic calendar listing would,
> however, be absolutely amazing and greatly appreciated.
>
> We'll definitely keep your rate card on file and reach out again once we have
> a larger marketing budget for future events.
>
> Best wishes,
> Carla

The shape: thank, state the real reason in one sentence, take the free thing
warmly and specifically, leave the door open. No apology spiral, no invented
excuse.

## Chasing money that is owed

> Hi there, I never received a response, is it possible to confirm please?
> Many thanks, Carla

Short, unbothered, still polite. She does not escalate in tone.
