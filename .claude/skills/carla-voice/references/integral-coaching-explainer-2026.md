# "What is Integral Coaching?" – her own explainer

Sent by Carla to new coaching clients, July–August 2026. Reproduced verbatim;
recipient names removed. This is her explaining her own practice in her own
words, unedited by anyone – the most useful sample we have for the coaching
pages.

---

A few people have asked me what Integral Coaching is and why I chose this
approach, so here's a simple explanation:

**What is Integral Coaching?**

Integral Coaching starts from a deceptively simple premise: Human beings are not
problems to be solved. We are complex, meaning-making systems.

When an Integral Coach works with someone, they aren't only asking: "What do you
want to achieve?"

They're also asking: "Who are you being while you pursue it? What way of seeing
the world is producing this result? And what new capacities need to be developed
for lasting change to occur?"

**What does it mean to view someone holistically?**

Most coaching focuses on one dimension:

- Behaviour ("Just do the thing.")
- Mindset ("Change your thoughts.")
- Goals ("Here's your action plan.")
- Emotions ("How does that make you feel?")

Integral Coaching says: All of these dimensions are interconnected.

The key question becomes: What way of being is producing the current results in
your life? And then: What new way of being wants to emerge?

**This is why Integral Coaching is developmental.**

It isn't trying to:

❌ fix you
❌ "optimise" you
❌ "hack" your productivity

Instead, it helps you:

✅ see yourself more clearly
✅ understand the wisdom and limitations of your current patterns
✅ cultivate new capacities
✅ become someone who can naturally create different outcomes.

**What does a 12-session container offer?**

Over 12 sessions, we work together in a structured developmental journey that
includes:

- Clarifying what you most want and where you feel stuck.
- Identifying the patterns, beliefs, and habits that keep you there.
- Developing new ways of being, thinking, and acting.
- Integrating practical experiments and practices into everyday life.
- Building sustainable change that continues beyond our work together.

The container provides enough time for real transformation. Instead of simply
solving a problem, we cultivate new capacities that support you long after the
coaching ends.

**Why did I choose Integral Coaching? / About me**

At the heart of Carla's work is a lifelong fascination with a single question:
How do human beings make meaning of their lives?

This question has guided both her academic and professional journey. She began
by studying symbols and visual culture through an undergraduate degree in
History of Art, before deepening her understanding of language, narrative, and
identity through a Master's degree in English Literature at the University of
Stellenbosch and an international exchange in Gender and Women's Studies at the
University of Utrecht in the Netherlands.

As her interests evolved, she completed a second Honours degree in Brand
Strategy at Vega School, exploring how meaning is created collectively through
communication and shared narratives.

Over the past decade, her work in adult learning design and executive education
has become another expression of this same inquiry: understanding how people
learn, lead, and transform themselves and the worlds they inhabit. Most
recently, she completed the Coaching for Development and Professional Coaching
programme through the Centre for Coaching at the University of Cape Town
Graduate School of Business, bringing her exploration of meaning-making into the
realm of human development and personal transformation.

Alongside her professional work, Carla is the founder of Modern Muse, a practice
dedicated to helping people navigate life's transitions through tarot,
symbolism, and reflective conversation. Her approach brings together deep
listening, ritual, coaching, mythology, and contemplative practice to create
spaces where people feel genuinely seen, held, and understood.

Whether working with leaders in organisations or individuals at moments of
profound transition, the thread running through all of Carla's work is a deep
belief that the stories and symbols we use to understand our lives have the
power to shape who we become – and that life's most significant transitions
deserve to be met with beauty, dignity, care, and meaning.
