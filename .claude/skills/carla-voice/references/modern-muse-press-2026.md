Hello, I just wanted to put the following on your radar!

**Tarot, Tequila & Winter Magic: Cape Town's Cult-Favourite "Cursed Thursdays"
Returns**

Cursed Thursdays – Cape Town's beloved underground tarot night – is officially
returning for winter 2026, bringing with it an intoxicating mix of cocktails and
cosmic guidance.

After a summer spent reading cards at festivals, weddings, and private events
across the city, the Cursed Thursdays collective is regrouping just as the
colder months begin settling over Cape Town. As the nights grow longer and the
city shifts into its darker, moodier season, guests are invited to gather once
again for an evening of tarot, connection, storytelling, and playful mysticism.

This season also welcomes a brand new sponsor: Inzalo Agave – makers of
small-batch agave spirits crafted from 100% wild red agave, wild yeast, and
mountain water.

"Tequila and tarot felt like a dangerously good idea," says founding member
Carla le Roux. "Cursed Thursdays has always been less about predicting the
future and more about creating a space where people can reflect, laugh, flirt,
unravel slightly, and reconnect with themselves and each other."

Blending elements of ritual and psychological insight, Cursed Thursdays has
quietly built a cult following in Cape Town among creatives, seekers, romantics,
and the spiritually curious.

Guests can expect:

- Live tarot readings
- Winter agave cocktails
- Unexpected conversations and mild existential revelations

Come cozy up at the gin bar and see what the universe has been trying to tell
you.
